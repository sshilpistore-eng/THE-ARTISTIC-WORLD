Relationships on the wheel

Complementary colors sit opposite each other and create the strongest contrast. Analogous colors sit close together and feel harmonious. Triadic colors are spaced evenly, giving balance with more variety than a complementary pair.

Classic pigment pairs

Red + blue → violet. Blue + yellow → green. Red + yellow → orange. Mixing two complements near 50/50 tends toward muted, earthy tones rather than pure grey — useful for shadow color instead of just going darker.
