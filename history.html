Literature & broader culture

Art rarely moves alone — these are the movements it shared a moment with.

Renaissance humanism

Alongside Renaissance painting, writers like Petrarch and Erasmus revived classical texts and reframed the individual as a subject worth studying closely.

Romantic poetry

Wordsworth, Byron and Shelley pursued the same interest in emotion and the natural sublime that Romantic painters explored on canvas.

Modernist fiction

Writers such as Woolf and Joyce experimented with form and perspective in the novel at the same time Cubism and Fauvism reshaped painting.
